How I work

Make the hard
parts clear.

The stack depends on the job. I start by figuring out what the problem really is, make the smart parts easy to follow, and keep working until the product is reliable.

01

Product engineering

I usually reach for Next.js, React, and TypeScript. I also work on the less visible parts: backends, authentication, and the details that make an app hold together.

02

AI and machine learning

I use agents, NLP, forecasting, and traditional machine learning when they fit the problem. I test the output and plan for what happens when a model gets it wrong.

03

Data work

I build dashboards, engineer features, and explain the results so the work is useful outside a notebook.
